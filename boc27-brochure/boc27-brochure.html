About the Conference

Where competition law meets the realities of business

Business of Competition returns to Prague in April 2027 for its second edition, bringing together competition lawyers, economists, regulators, in-house counsel, litigation funders and other professionals working across European competition law.

The conference is built around practical discussion — current cases, enforcement priorities, litigation strategy, economics and the business consequences of competition law developments.

Following the 2026 edition, the programme will expand the involvement of corporate counsel and continue to bring together different sides of the market for two days of focused discussions and meaningful networking.

Preliminary Programme · Themes for 2027

Day One

Enforcement, Markets & Business

Themes under discussion for the first day. Session titles, timings and speakers will be confirmed as the programme develops.

  • 01

    European Competition Policy in 2027

    What is changing in enforcement priorities, institutional strategy and the relationship between competition policy and broader EU economic objectives?

  • 02

    Cartels & Enforcement: Where are authorities focusing now?

    Current investigations, evidence, leniency, cooperation and the practical consequences for businesses and advisers.

  • 03

    Competition Law Inside the Business

    How in-house teams manage competition risk, investigations, commercial pressure and increasingly complex regulatory expectations.

  • 04

    Digital Markets, Platforms & Competition

    DMA enforcement, marketplaces, pricing, self-preferencing and the evolving relationship between competition law and platform regulation.

  • 05

    Headline Business Session Topic to be announced

    A current market issue examined from the perspectives of business, regulators, lawyers and economists.

04  Business of Competition 2027
Preliminary Programme · Themes for 2027

Day Two

Litigation, Damages & Economics

Themes under discussion for the second day, moving from the courtroom to the economics behind it.

  • 06

    The Business of Antitrust Litigation

    What drives major competition claims — case selection, jurisdiction, funding, evidence, strategy and settlement?

  • 07

    Private Enforcement: Where is competition damages litigation heading?

    Developments across European jurisdictions and what recent cases tell us about the next wave of claims.

  • 08

    Can economics determine the outcome of a competition case?

    Damages, causation, counterfactuals, pass-on, data and the practical role economists play in litigation and investigations.

  • 09

    AI, Algorithms & the Next Competition Questions

    How AI, algorithmic pricing and new technologies are changing the competition risk landscape.

  • 10

    What happens when the investigation ends?

    From authority decision to litigation, damages exposure, reputational consequences and business response.
